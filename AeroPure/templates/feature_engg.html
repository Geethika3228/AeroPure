Feature Engineering Roadmap

Based on the insights from EDA, these are the preprocessing steps planned before training a forecasting model.

  • 1
    Handle Missing Values
    Air-quality sensors drop readings; impute or interpolate gaps in PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NO2, CO, O3, TEMP, PRES, DEWP, RAIN, WSPM per station.
  • 2
    Encode Categorical Variables
    Convert wind direction (wd) and station into numeric form (one-hot / cyclical encoding for wind direction).
  • 3
    Time-Based Features
    Derive hour-of-day, day-of-week, month, and season from the datetime column to capture daily/seasonal pollution cycles.
  • 4
    Lag & Rolling Features
    Create lagged PM2.5 values (t-1, t-24) and rolling averages, since air quality is highly autocorrelated over time.
  • 5
    Scale Numerical Features
    Standardize pollutant concentrations and weather variables so they contribute fairly to the model.
  • 6
    Treat Outliers
    Review extreme pollutant spikes flagged by the EDA box plots — some are real pollution events, others are sensor errors.
  • 7
    Feature Selection
    Use the correlation heatmap to drop redundant weather/pollutant pairs and keep the strongest predictors of PM2.5.
